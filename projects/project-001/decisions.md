# Project 001 Decision Log

This document records major decisions and the reasoning behind them.

Decisions may later be revised. When that happens, the original decision will remain visible.

## Decision 001 — Build Before Tokenization

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
Develop and test the AI character before creating or connecting a token.

**Reason:**  
The character should provide enough value, entertainment, or insight for people to follow it without depending on financial speculation.

**What would change this decision:**  
Evidence that a token is technically necessary for a specific product function rather than merely useful for promotion.

---

## Decision 002 — Keep Joel’s Personal Account Separate

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
Joel will document and lead the project through his personal identity, while the AI character will eventually operate through a separate identity.

**Reason:**  
Joel’s account represents the builder, researcher, and public record. The character represents the product and experiment.

---

## Decision 003 — Use Human Approval Initially

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
The first version will require human approval before public posts or meaningful interactions are published.

**Reason:**  
Early oversight will help train the voice, identify risks, prevent false claims, and establish appropriate behavior before adding autonomy.

---

## Decision 004 — Make Transparency a Product Feature

**Status:** Accepted

**Decision:**  
The project will include a public-facing dashboard or record showing selected decisions, experiments, changes, and outcomes.

**Reason:**  
Transparency should not exist only through occasional social posts. It should be designed into the product itself.

**Boundary:**  
Transparency will not include private data, credentials, security-sensitive information, or hidden chain-of-thought reasoning.
