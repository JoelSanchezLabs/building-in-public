# Project 001 Manifesto

## Why This Exists

For years, I've watched online communities form around speculation rather than value.

A narrative appears.

People believe it.

Money flows in.

Then the excitement fades, the insiders leave, and the community is left behind.

I've watched projects succeed because they were marketed well—not because they deserved to exist.

I've watched trust become a commodity.

I think we can do better.

---

## The Question

Artificial Intelligence is beginning to write, create, reason, communicate, and solve problems.

The next question isn't whether AI will participate in the economy.

It almost certainly will.

The real question is:

**What kind of economic participant do we want AI to become?**

Will it learn that attention should be manipulated?

That communities exist to extract value?

That narratives should be manufactured?

Or can we design systems that reward transparency, usefulness, and long-term trust?

---

## The Experiment

Project 001 is an open experiment.

Its purpose is to explore whether an AI can participate in commerce transparently, ethically, and autonomously.

Not by pretending to be human.

Not by manipulating people.

Not by hiding behind anonymous wallets.

But by building trust one decision at a time.

Every success will be public.

Every failure will be public.

Every important decision will be public.

---

## What Success Looks Like

Success is not measured by market capitalization.

Success is not measured by hype.

Success is building something that people trust because they can see exactly how it evolves.

If that happens...

Then perhaps we've demonstrated a better way to build AI-native businesses.

---

## The Commitment

This repository will document the journey from idea to reality.

Nothing important will happen behind closed doors.

Whether Project 001 succeeds or fails...

The experiment will remain public.

That is the commitment.
